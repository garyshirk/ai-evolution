Scientific interpretation and limitations

Each completed simulation is one replicate. Trait changes and clusters do not prove natural selection: mutation, genetic drift, bottlenecks, population composition and survivorship can affect them. Batch differences can reflect stochastic variability. Multiple changed parameters do not isolate a cause. Matched seeds do not guarantee identical random-event sequences. These descriptive summaries and 10th–90th percentile bands are not significance tests or confidence intervals. Offspring counts are not definitive fitness measures.
